NO-RAG BASELINE ANSWER
================================================================================
The E2AP (E2 Application Protocol) defines several services for communication between gNodeBs and the E2 node. The main services include:

1. E2 Setup
2. E2 Modification
3. E2 Release
4. E2 Status Reporting
5. E2 Notification

These services facilitate the establishment, modification, and termination of connections, as well as the reporting of status and notifications between the gNodeB and the E2 node.
